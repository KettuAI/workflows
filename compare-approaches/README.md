Use it when a technical choice is better settled with evidence than with opinions: two libraries, two data models, two algorithms or two architectures.

1. Frame states the decision, both approaches and how to judge them. You approve it.
2. Approach A and Approach B are prototyped at the same time, each on its own branch, often by different agents.
3. Compare measures both against the criteria and recommends one. You approve the conclusion.

The prototypes are throwaway work for learning; the decision is the result.
